01 / ABOUT HASSAN

Hey, I'm Hassan Shama — an Egypt-based creative designer exploring the intersection of graphic design, visual storytelling and AI. My journey started with Photoshop in 2016 and grew into freelance work for local and international clients. Since 2023, I've taken on creative projects alongside my career in nursing. I believe good design makes ideas easier to feel, understand and remember.
